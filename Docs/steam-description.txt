Vanilla Combat Overhaul makes fights more tactical, readable and skill-driven while keeping RimWorld's familiar combat at its core.

[h1]Melee with momentum[/h1]
[list]
[*]Armed pawns can parry attacks and answer a successful parry with a counter-attack.
[*]Attacking from the side or rear matters, while surrounded defenders become easier to overwhelm.
[*]Attack direction and height influence where blows land.
[/list]

[h1]More expressive firefights[/h1]
[list]
[*]Skilled shooters handle inaccurate weapons and bad weather more effectively.
[*]Moving targets are harder to hit, and misses spread further at long range.
[*]Visible projectile trails make incoming fire easier to read.
[*]Drafted shooters can switch between Precision, Short Burst and Suppression fire, or let Auto choose by distance. Enemies pick their own.
[*]Incoming fire suppresses: pawns under sustained fire aim worse and slower, and pinned enemies dive for cover. Bullets flying past count too, armour shrugs off rounds that cannot match it, and being under fire hurts mood. Works with any modded gun, no patches needed.
[*]Bullets and arrows produce distinct wounds based on how they strike the target.
[/list]

[h1]Armour and equipment[/h1]
[list]
[*]Strong armour can stop weak hits outright, while partial protection still reduces their impact.
[*]Loadouts, assigned in their own Assign tab column, set which weapons each colonist uses, whether they carry a backup melee weapon, and which items they keep in their inventory.
[*]Colonists choose and upgrade their primary weapon by skill, switch to their melee sidearm when an enemy reaches them, and switch back afterwards.
[*]Manual weapon choices remain locked until you allow automatic upgrades again.
[*]Improved apparel coverage gives gloves, boots, masks, helmets and glasses more sensible protection and layering.
[/list]

Combat and equipment systems can be configured individually, so you can keep the parts that suit your colony.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
Designed for vanilla-style combat and standard weapon and apparel content. Not compatible with Combat Extended, Yayo's Combat, Vanilla Combat Reloaded, Vanilla Fire Modes or Auto Arm because they control overlapping combat or equipment systems. With Simple Sidearms active, Simple Sidearms handles backup weapons and loadouts handle the rest. With another loadout mod, turn Loadouts off in the settings.
