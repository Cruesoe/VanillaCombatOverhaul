[b]Deeper, more tactical combat that keeps RimWorld's own weapons, armour and rules.[/b]

The depth of Combat Extended without its ammo, calibres or compatibility patches: every system reads vanilla stats, so modded weapons and apparel work as they are.

[h2]Melee[/h2]
[list]
[*]Armed pawns parry incoming blows, and a successful parry earns a counter-attack.
[*]Flanking matters: attacks from behind cannot be parried, and a surrounded defender is soon overwhelmed.
[*]Skilled shooters caught in melee sometimes fire point-blank instead of swinging.
[/list]

[h2]Shooting[/h2]
[list]
[*]Shooting skill counts for more, overcoming bad weapons and weather, while moving targets are harder to hit.
[*]Misses spread realistically around the target, and glowing tracers show where fire is coming from.
[*]Drafted shooters choose Precision, Short Burst or Suppression fire, or let Auto pick by distance.
[*]Skilled marksmen can aim for the head, torso or legs, and hits land on the side the shot came from.
[*]Bullets and arrows wound differently: rounds fragment, pass through or mushroom, and arrows cut deep.
[/list]

[h2]Suppression[/h2]
[list]
[*]Bullets landing near or flying past a pawn suppress them, making them aim worse and slower.
[*]Sustained fire pins enemies, sending them diving for cover.
[*]Armour shrugs off rounds that cannot match it, and being under fire hurts mood.
[/list]

[h2]Armour and gear[/h2]
[list]
[*]Good armour stops weak hits outright instead of turning them into bruises.
[*]Gloves, boots, masks, helmets and glasses protect the parts they cover and layer sensibly.
[*]Loadouts in the Assign tab set each colonist's weapons, a backup melee weapon, medicine and other supplies.
[*]Colonists upgrade their weapons by skill and draw their sidearm when an enemy reaches them.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Every system can be switched on or off individually, so you keep only the parts that suit your colony.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Works with vanilla-style weapon, apparel and race mods without patches. With [b]Progression: Ammunition[/b], loadouts carry the refills each colonist's weapon needs. With [b]Simple Sidearms[/b], it handles backup weapons and loadouts handle the rest; with another loadout mod, turn Loadouts off.
Not compatible with Combat Extended, Yayo's Combat, Vanilla Combat Reloaded, Vanilla Fire Modes or Auto Arm, because they control the same systems.
Safe to add to an existing save, though a new game is recommended (see below).

[h2]New game recommended[/h2]
Existing saves load fine: old weapon policies become loadouts and colonists keep their medicine settings. But combat plays very differently, and a colony armed and armoured for vanilla fights will feel the change, so a fresh start gives the best experience.
