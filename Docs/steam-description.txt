[b]Deeper, more tactical combat that keeps RimWorld's own weapons, armor and rules.[/b]

The depth of Combat Extended without its ammo, calibers or compatibility patches: every system reads vanilla stats, so modded weapons and apparel work as they are. The mod is still being tuned, so if a fight feels off or something breaks, please leave a comment with what happened and, for errors, the red text from the log.

[h2]Melee[/h2]
[list]
[*]Armed pawns parry incoming blows, and a successful parry earns a counter-attack.
[*]Flanking matters: attacks from behind cannot be parried, and a surrounded defender is soon overwhelmed.
[*]Skilled shooters caught in melee sometimes fire point-blank instead of swinging.
[/list]

[h2]Shooting[/h2]
[list]
[*]Shooting skill counts for more, overcoming bad weapons and weather, while moving targets are harder to hit.
[*]Misses spread realistically around the target, and glowing tracers show where fire is coming from.
[*]Drafted shooters choose Precision, Short Burst or Suppression fire, or let Auto pick by distance.
[*]Run and gun: shooters fire on the move instead of stopping, at a small cost to accuracy and pace.
[*]Skilled shooters and fighters can aim for the head, torso or legs, and hits land on the side the attack came from.
[*]Bullets and arrows wound differently: rounds from any gun fragment, pass through or mushroom, while arrows and thrown pila cut deep.
[/list]

[h2]Suppression[/h2]
[list]
[*]Bullets landing near or flying past a pawn suppress them, making them aim worse and slower.
[*]Sustained fire pins pawns, sending them diving for cover and flat on the ground.
[*]Armor shrugs off rounds that cannot match it, and being under fire hurts mood.
[/list]

[h2]Armor and gear[/h2]
[list]
[*]Good armor stops weak hits outright instead of turning them into bruises.
[*]Gloves, boots, masks, helmets and glasses protect the parts they cover and layer sensibly.
[*]Loadouts in the Assign tab set each colonist's weapons, a backup melee weapon, medicine and other supplies.
[*]Colonists upgrade their weapons by skill and draw their sidearm when an enemy reaches them.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Every system can be switched on or off individually, so you keep only the parts that suit your colony.
[list]
[*][b]Melee[/b]: parrying, counter-attacks and point-blank shots.
[*][b]Damage and hit location[/b]: hits by side, melee flanking and aiming at a height.
[*][b]Ranged[/b]: skill-based accuracy, evasion, miss spread, tracers and run and gun, which can be limited to your colonists.
[*][b]Suppression[/b]: pinning, the mood penalty and overall strength.
[*][b]Fire modes[/b]: whether other factions use them, the Auto distances, and each mode's accuracy, aiming time, recovery and burst size.
[*][b]Armor[/b], [b]Projectile wounds[/b], [b]Apparel and coverage[/b] and [b]Loadouts[/b] can each be turned off as a whole.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Works with vanilla-style weapon, apparel and race mods without patches. With [b]Progression: Ammunition[/b], loadouts carry the refills each colonist's weapon needs. With [b]Dubs Mint Menus[/b], its meal and drug carry settings appear in each loadout's supplies. With [b]Simple Sidearms[/b], it handles backup weapons and loadouts handle the rest; with another loadout mod, turn Loadouts off.
Not compatible with Combat Extended, Yayo's Combat, Vanilla Combat Reloaded, Vanilla Fire Modes, Auto Arm, Suppression (Continued), Misstall's Combat Tweaks or Run and Gun, because they control the same systems.
Safe to add to an existing save: old weapon policies become loadouts and colonists keep their medicine settings. A new game is still recommended, as a colony armed and armored for vanilla combat will feel the change.
